Dolce vita and French art de vivre





Abraccetto, nestled between the Pantheon and the Luxembourg Gardens, invites you to an experience marked by elegance. In a refined setting, the authentic flavors of Italy blossom through dishes carefully prepared using fresh ingredients.

Our culinary adventure is the fruit of a harmonious collaboration between a dedicated and passionate Franco-Italian team, always eager to go further to satisfy our customers.

Whether for a romantic dinner, a business lunch or a family gathering, our restaurant offers a friendly and sophisticated environment, where la dolce vita meets the French art of living. We invite you to discover our passion for cuisine, our attentive service and our warm atmosphere.

Frequently asked questions

What are A Braccetto's opening hours?
A Braccetto is open every day with continuous service, Monday to Friday from 10:00 am to 2:00 am, and Saturday and Sunday from 9:00 am to 2:00 am.
How can I book a table at A Braccetto?
Reservations are made online from the abraccettoparis.com website. Specific options are also available for brunch and the pizza tasting menu.
Does A Braccetto have a terrace?
Yes, A Braccetto has a terrace in Paris 5th, perfect for lunch, dinner or a cocktail in the Latin Quarter, a stone's throw from the Panthéon and the Luxembourg Gardens.
Does A Braccetto offer vegetarian options?
Yes, A Braccetto offers vegetarian dishes within its Italian cuisine: pizzas, pasta and antipasti. The restaurant is recognised as vegetarian-friendly.
